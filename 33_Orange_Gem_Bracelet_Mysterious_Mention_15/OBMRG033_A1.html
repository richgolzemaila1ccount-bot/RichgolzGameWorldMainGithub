33 Orange Gem Bracelet : Mysterious Mention 15



Orange Gem Bracelet : MM15


It seems you pressed the correct button.

The alphabet is matched with numbers starting from A.
A equals 1, B equals 2, all the way to Z equals 26.
The answer is the number of the alphabet with the smallest value in the word.
In other words, it is the letter closest to A in the alphabet.

For “like,” the letter E has the smallest value, so the answer is 5.
In the same way, for “share,” the smallest letter is A, which is 1.
Therefore, for “comment,” the smallest letter is C, so the answer is 3.

You press the 3 button beneath the frame.
Soon, a part of the wall beneath the buttons opens, and the Orange Bracelet treasure is revealed.

You take the treasure from the small space inside the opened section of the wall and secure it.

After obtaining the treasure, you leave the area.

You successfully escape the location.

You have secured the treasure.

Congratulations!
